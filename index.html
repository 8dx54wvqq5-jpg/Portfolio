◇ 03 · Principles
HOW I WORK
01

Predictable is trustworthy

In financial systems, consistency isn't an aesthetic preference. Every inconsistent pattern adds risk. Admins moving money need to know exactly what a click will do.

02

Transparency beats automation

AI earns trust when it shows its reasoning. On Disputes Workspace I shipped AI-assisted intake with confidence indicators, visible evidence, and a one-click way for a person to override it. I also kept AI out of systems where plain deterministic rules are safer to trust. Knowing which call to make is most of the job.

03

Structure before surface

The IA redesigns I've shipped moved usability more than any visual decision. Separate configuration from operations, then make it beautiful.

04

Accessible, accountable AI

Accessibility and AI ethics share the same root: systems that affect real people carry real responsibility. I build for WCAG compliance from day one. When AI is involved, bias must be visible, outcomes explainable, and human override always possible.
