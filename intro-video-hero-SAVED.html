◇ 03 · Principles
HOW I WORK
01

Predictable is trustworthy

In financial systems, consistency isn't an aesthetic preference — every inconsistent pattern adds risk. Admins moving money need to know exactly what a click will do.

02

Transparency beats automation

AI earns adoption when it shows its reasoning. I've shipped AI that explains itself, and deliberately kept AI out of systems where deterministic rules are more trustworthy.

03

Structure before surface

The IA redesigns I've shipped moved usability more than any visual decision. Separate configuration from operations, then make it beautiful.

04

Accessible, accountable AI

Accessibility and AI ethics share the same root: systems that affect real people carry real responsibility. I build for WCAG compliance from day one — and when AI is involved, bias must be visible, outcomes explainable, and human override always possible.
